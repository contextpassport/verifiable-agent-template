Article 12 of the EU AI Act requires providers of high-risk AI
systems to ensure their systems technically allow for the automatic
recording of events ("logs") over the lifetime of the system. The
records must be sufficient to identify risk situations under Article
79(1), facilitate post-market monitoring under Article 72, and
support operational monitoring under Article 26(5).

The Commission interprets "traceability" as a structural property:
the record itself must be tamper-evident and independently verifiable,
not merely a claim by the provider that records exist. A standard
database audit log inside the system being audited typically does not
satisfy this — it is a self-report, not an audit trail.

In practice, providers are implementing this with hash-chained,
signed records anchored to external transparency services. The
record format must be machine-readable, retain attribution to the
agent that produced it, and remain verifiable by an auditor or
regulator without depending on the provider's continued operation.

guidance on traceability]
